Design Process
    1. Define a goal
        - Should be only a sentence or two.
    2. Define requirements
        - What are the project's constraints/requirements?
        - "What?" not "how?"
    3. Define tools, targets & backup plan
        - Where can it be used?
        - Who is it for?
        - Backup to Google Drive or GitHub
    4. Separate problems
    5. Determine sequence
        - In what order do things happen?
    A. Add commented-out method calls to outline sequence
    B. Fill out one method at a time
    C. Test frequently
    D. Optimize for maintainability

Debugging
    1. Find the root cause of the problem.
    2. Understand why the issue is occurring.
    3. Determine how to fix the issue.
    4. Repair the issue causing the problem.
    5. Retest to ensure the problem has been fixed.
    6. Retest to ensure no new problems have emerged.
